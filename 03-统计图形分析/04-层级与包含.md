# 层级与包含｜图表机制分析

本组按读图任务整理。以下字段来自固定提交源码的 dimensions；“必”表示 required:true，“多”表示 multiple:true，未标必的字段仍可能是有意义绘图所必需。字段名用源码 id。分析、用途与限制是本库解释，不是 RAWGraphs 原文。

## 矩形树图（treemap）

- **数据结构**：多级分类路径×叶子权重。
- **字段约束**：hierarchy (number,date,string；必；多)；size (number)；color (number,date,string)；label (number,date,string；多)。
- **统计转换/算法**：按层级列 rollups 聚合叶权，hierarchy.sum 后 treemap 布局。
- **视觉映射**：嵌套矩形面积近似权重。
- **读图任务与边界**：父值为叶值合计；须防止总计行重复计入，留白影响精确面积。
- **源码**：[字段定义](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/treemap/dimensions.js) · [映射](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/treemap/mapping.js) · [绘制](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/treemap/render.js) · [视觉参数](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/treemap/visualOptions.js)

## 圆堆积图（circlepacking）

- **数据结构**：多级分类路径×叶子权重。
- **字段约束**：hierarchy (number,date,string；必；多)；size (number)；color (number,date,string)；label (number,date,string；多)。
- **统计转换/算法**：同层级聚合与 hierarchy.sum，pack 布局。
- **视觉映射**：叶圆面积、父圆包含关系。
- **读图任务与边界**：父圆包含空隙，面积不可直接当精确累计权重；非负叶权重。
- **源码**：[字段定义](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/circlepacking/dimensions.js) · [映射](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/circlepacking/mapping.js) · [绘制](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/circlepacking/render.js) · [视觉参数](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/circlepacking/visualOptions.js)

## 旭日图（sunburst）

- **数据结构**：多级分类路径×叶子权重。
- **字段约束**：hierarchy (number,date,string；必；多)；size (number)；color (number,date,string)；label (number,date,string；多)。
- **统计转换/算法**：同层级聚合，hierarchy.sum 后 partition。
- **视觉映射**：同环角宽表示份额，径向层数表示深度。
- **读图任务与边界**：跨环面积不宜直接比较；注意深层小扇区。
- **源码**：[字段定义](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/sunburst/dimensions.js) · [映射](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/sunburst/mapping.js) · [绘制](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/sunburst/render.js) · [视觉参数](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/sunburst/visualOptions.js)

## 树状图（dendrogram）

- **数据结构**：多级分类路径×可选叶权重。
- **字段约束**：hierarchy (number,date,string；必；多)；size (number)；color (number,date,string)；label (number,date,string；多)。
- **统计转换/算法**：同层级聚合，d3.tree/cluster，叶权重可映射sqrt节点半径。
- **视觉映射**：节点、连接线、层级位置。
- **读图任务与边界**：表达已知分类，不是由相似度自动推断的聚类树。
- **源码**：[字段定义](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/dendrogram/dimensions.js) · [映射](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/dendrogram/mapping.js) · [绘制](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/dendrogram/render.js) · [视觉参数](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/dendrogram/visualOptions.js)

## 径向树状图（circularDendrogram）

- **数据结构**：多级分类路径×可选叶权重。
- **字段约束**：hierarchy (number,date,string；必；多)；size (number)；color (number,date,string)；label (number,date,string；多)。
- **统计转换/算法**：同层级聚合，tree/cluster 的极坐标布局。
- **视觉映射**：中心到外侧表示层级、线表示父子、叶圆可编码权重。
- **读图任务与边界**：角距离通常由布局决定，不是相似度。
- **源码**：[字段定义](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/circularDendrogram/dimensions.js) · [映射](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/circularDendrogram/mapping.js) · [绘制](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/circularDendrogram/render.js) · [视觉参数](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/circularDendrogram/visualOptions.js)

## Voronoi 树图（voronoitreemap）

- **数据结构**：多级分类路径×叶权重。
- **字段约束**：hierarchy (number,date,string；必；多)；size (number)；color (number,date,string)；label (number,date,string；多)。
- **统计转换/算法**：同层级聚合，hierarchy.sum 后 voronoiTreemap 迭代布局，可裁剪多边形。
- **视觉映射**：嵌套不规则区域面积近似权重。
- **读图任务与边界**：迭代结果和边界影响误差及可比性；不代表真实地图。
- **源码**：[字段定义](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/voronoitreemap/dimensions.js) · [映射](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/voronoitreemap/mapping.js) · [绘制](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/voronoitreemap/render.js) · [视觉参数](https://github.com/rawgraphs/rawgraphs-charts/blob/cade3b6e1b4d35d365440b1365d51b0dd48c9bce/src/voronoitreemap/visualOptions.js)
